<p class="gym-sub">Stretch warm, most days. That is how you get flexible.</p>

<p class="gym-h2">The rule</p>
<div class="callout"><b>Frequency beats duration.</b> A little most days beats one long session. Around ten minutes per muscle a week.</div>

<p class="gym-h2">Warm up, before a lift or run</p>
<div class="card">
<table class="ex">
<tr><td>Leg swings</td><td class="r">10 each way</td></tr>
<tr><td>Walking lunges</td><td class="r">10</td></tr>
<tr><td>Hip circles</td><td class="r">10 each way</td></tr>
<tr><td>Arm circles</td><td class="r">10 each way</td></tr>
<tr><td>Bodyweight squats</td><td class="r">10</td></tr>
</table>
</div>

<p class="gym-h2">How to do it</p>
<div class="card rules">
<div class="rule"><span class="k">Warm</span><span class="v">Never cold. After a session, or a few minutes of the warm up.</span></div>
<div class="rule"><span class="k">Hold</span><span class="v">30 seconds each. Breathe out and ease deeper. Mild tension, never pain.</span></div>
<div class="rule"><span class="k">Order</span><span class="v">Dynamic before, static after. Long holds before training dull your legs.</span></div>
<div class="rule"><span class="k">Daily</span><span class="v">Hips and hamstrings every day, the full set after each session.</span></div>
</div>
