## What it is

A VCV rack 2 module that draws audio reactive visuals in the background. This replaces your background with projectM/Milkdrop visuals and you can adjust the sensitivity via the patch input and knobs. The visualisations can be cycled through using the buttons on the module.

![Pure Dreams visuals reacting to the audio](/images/pure-dreams/demo.webp)


## How it's built

Written entirely in C++ and currently going through the testing phase before it is offically released on the VCV library as I'm currently ironing out some small compatitbility issues. Testsed and works on Mac, Windows and Linux.
