<h1>Stifado</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Greek beef and shallot stew, 3 hr braise, serves 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">The stew</span>
stewing beef     1kg, in large chunks
baby onions      600g, peeled, left whole
garlic           4 cloves, sliced
chopped tomatoes 1 x 400g tin
beef stock       400ml
red wine         250ml
red wine vinegar 2 tbsp
tomato puree     1 tbsp
olive oil        2 tbsp

<span style="color: #888;">The spice</span>
cinnamon stick   1
bay leaves       4
dried oregano    1 tbsp
ground allspice  1 pinch
ground cloves    1 pinch
caster sugar     1 pinch
salt and pepper  to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Shin or chuck. A cut with some fat and collagen goes meltingly tender over the long braise, where lean cuts stay tough.<br>Peeling that many small onions is the only faff. Cover them in just boiled water for a minute and the skins slip off.<br>Better the next day, once the spice settles and the sauce thickens, so a good one to make ahead.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 160°C (140°C fan). Warm the oil in the Dutch oven over medium high heat and brown the beef in batches, then lift it out.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Do not crowd the pot. In one layer with space the meat browns; piled in it steams and stays grey. Browning is most of the flavour.</span>

**2.** Turn the heat down, add the whole onions, and colour them for 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic for a minute.

**3.** Add the tomato puree, cinnamon, bay, oregano, allspice, cloves and sugar. Stir for a minute to wake the spices up.

**4.** Pour in the vinegar and wine, scraping the bottom, and let it bubble for 2 minutes. Add the tomatoes, stock, and the browned beef with its juices. Season.

**5.** Bring to a gentle simmer, lid on, and into the oven for about 2 hours, until the beef pulls apart with a fork and the onions are soft.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Check once near the end. If the sauce is thin, finish it on the hob with the lid off for 10 minutes to reduce.</span>

**6.** Fish out the cinnamon stick and bay leaves and taste for salt.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: cool completely, then portion into bags or tubs.<br>Reheat: from frozen on a low heat with the lid on, or thaw in the fridge first.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen, 3 days in the fridge.</span>
