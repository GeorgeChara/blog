<h1>Aji Verde</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">5 min, blend</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">coriander       large bunch
green chilli    1-2
garlic          1 clove
lime            1
rapeseed oil    4 tbsp
salt            to taste
msg (optional)  1/4 tsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The Peruvian green sauce served with charcoal grilled chicken. Use the coriander stalks as well as the leaves, they carry most of the flavour.<br>A spoon of aji amarillo paste deepens it and makes it more authentic, if you can find it.<br>A pinch of MSG with the salt rounds out the savouriness and makes the herbs and lime pop. Start small and taste.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Blend the coriander, chilli, garlic, lime juice and salt until broken down.

**2.** Stream in the rapeseed oil with the blender running until it emulsifies into a smooth, vivid green sauce. Loosen with a splash of water if it is too thick.

**3.** Taste and adjust with more salt and lime.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">For the creamier restaurant style, blend in a spoon of mayo or greek yoghurt in place of some of the oil. That takes it close to a chilli coriander version of <a href="/cookbook/parsley-yoghurt/">parsley yoghurt</a>.<br>Best with grilled chicken or <a href="/cookbook/souvla/">souvla</a>.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Keeps 4 to 5 days in a sealed jar in the fridge. The top darkens as the herbs oxidise, just stir it back in. Don't leave it out at room temperature, the fresh garlic and herbs won't hold.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: in an ice cube tray, then bag the cubes.<br>Thaw: a cube or two as needed, or stir frozen into something hot.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen, colour dulls a little but the flavour holds.</span>
