<h1>Banana Bread</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">60 min bake, 170°C, 2lb loaf tin, makes 1</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">ripe bananas       400g (3–4)
butter             115g
soft brown sugar   150g
eggs               2
plain yogurt       3 tbsp
vanilla            1 tsp
plain flour        250g
bicarbonate soda   1 tsp
salt               1/2 tsp
cinnamon           1 tsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The blacker and softer the bananas, the sweeter the loaf. In a hurry, bake them unpeeled at 130°C for 15 min until the skins go black.<br>Brown the butter first for a nutty, caramel depth. The single biggest flavour upgrade.<br>Yogurt keeps it moist, sour cream works too. Cinnamon is optional.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 170°C and line a 2lb loaf tin with paper.

**2.** Melt the butter. For the best flavour, keep it on the heat until it foams, smells nutty and turns golden brown, then leave to cool a little.

**3.** Mash the bananas in a big bowl until fairly smooth.

**4.** Whisk into the bananas until well combined:

{{< ingr "butter=115g melted, soft brown sugar=150g, eggs=2, plain yogurt=3 tbsp, vanilla=1 tsp" >}}

**5.** Add the dry ingredients and fold with a spatula just until no dry flour remains. Stop there, overmixing is the one thing that toughens the loaf:

{{< ingr "plain flour=250g, bicarbonate soda=1 tsp, salt=1/2 tsp, cinnamon=1 tsp" >}}

**6.** Pour into the tin. Bake 55–65 min until deep golden and a skewer comes out with only a few moist crumbs. If the top browns before the centre sets, lay foil over it halfway.

**7.** Cool in the tin 10 min, then turn out onto a rack. It slices best fully cool.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Fold in 100g chocolate chunks or toasted walnuts at the end of step 5 if you like.<br>Keeps 3–4 days wrapped, or freezes well, whole or in slices.</span>
