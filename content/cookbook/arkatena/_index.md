<h1>Arkatena</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">overnight poolish, 50 min bake, 185°C, makes 1 loaf</p>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Real arkatena rises on arkatis, a wild foam from fermented chickpeas. Tastes great, but slow and a faff. This is the shortcut: a chickpea poolish with a pinch of yeast, the same spiced water, the same mastic and mahlepi. Dense, tight crumb, sesame all over.</span>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Spiced water (night before)</span>
water                350g
bay leaves           3
black peppercorns    3g
ginger               15g
sugar                4g

<span style="color: #888;">Poolish (night before)</span>
chickpea flour       60g
strong white flour   90g
spiced water         200g
fast action yeast    1g

<span style="color: #888;">Dough</span>
strong white flour   300g
spiced water         90g
salt                 8g
olive oil            30g
ground mahlepi       1g
ground mastic        0.5g
nutmeg               0.5g
fast action yeast    1g

<span style="color: #888;">To top</span>
white sesame seeds   20g
black sesame seeds   5g
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Chickpea flour is gram or besan flour, from any Asian or Middle Eastern shop.<br>Mastic and mahlepi are at Cypriot, Greek or Middle Eastern grocers. Grind the mastic tears with a pinch of the sugar so they do not gum up.<br>Use bottled or filtered water, chlorine holds back the rise.</span>

## Recipe

<span style="color: #888;">Night before</span>

**1.** Make the spiced water. Boil the water with the bay, peppercorns, ginger and sugar for a minute. Steep until lukewarm, then strain. You need about 290g.

**2.** Stir the chickpea flour, 90g strong flour, 200g of the spiced water and 1g yeast to a thick batter. Cover and leave somewhere warm 12 to 16 hours, until bubbly. Save the rest of the spiced water for tomorrow.

<span style="color: #888;">Next day</span>

**1.** Put the poolish, 300g strong flour, salt, olive oil, mahlepi, mastic, nutmeg, the last 1g yeast and 90g spiced water in a stand mixer. Knead with the dough hook, low to bring together, then medium 6 to 8 minutes, until smooth. Keep it firm, that gives the tight crumb. By hand, knead 10 minutes.

**2.** Cover and prove until doubled, 1.5 to 2 hours.

**3.** Shape into a 28cm oblong. Brush with water and roll all over in the sesame.

**4.** Put on a lined tray, slash deep across the top to mark the slices, and prove 45 minutes to 1 hour. Start heating the baking stone now, if using.

**5.** Bake at 185°C for 45 to 50 minutes, until deep golden and hollow underneath.

**6.** Cool fully before slicing.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Bake on a flat tray, not a Dutch oven. You want the sesame to toast and the crust to stay firm, not steam soft under a lid.<br>Better still with a baking stone. Heat it in the oven for 30 to 45 minutes first, then slide the loaf on its paper straight onto the hot stone, for a crisper, more even base.<br>For the real thing, swap the poolish for arkatis: steep coarsely ground chickpeas in the warm spiced water 12 to 16 hours until a thick foam forms, and leaven with that instead of yeast.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: wrap the whole loaf well, or freeze in slices.<br>Reheat: thaw at room temperature, or refresh in a warm oven.<br>Keeps: dense and long lasting, several days in a tin, 3 months frozen.</span>
