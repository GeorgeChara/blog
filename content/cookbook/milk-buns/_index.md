<h1>Milk Buns</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">16 min bake, 180°C (160°C fan), makes 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Tangzhong</span>
strong white flour  20g
milk                100g

<span style="color: #888;">Dough</span>
strong white flour  350g
caster sugar        30g
fast action yeast   6g
salt                7g
milk                150g
egg                 1
unsalted butter     30g

<span style="color: #888;">To finish</span>
milk                to brush
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Softer and less sweet than <a href="/cookbook/brioche-buns/">brioche</a>. Shape into logs for sub rolls, or rounds for burgers.<br>Brushed with milk, not egg, so the crust stays soft and pale.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Whisk the tangzhong flour and milk in a small pan. Heat, stirring, until it thickens to a paste. Cool.

**2.** In a stand mixer with the dough hook, mix the dough flour, sugar, yeast and salt. Add the milk, egg and tangzhong. Knead on low to bring together, then medium 5 minutes.

**3.** Add the butter a bit at a time, then knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth.

**4.** Cover and prove until doubled, 1 to 1.5 hours.

**5.** Divide into 6. Shape into 15cm logs for sub rolls, or tight balls for burgers. Space on a lined tray and prove 1 hour until puffy.

**6.** Brush with milk. Bake at 180°C (160°C fan) for 15 to 18 minutes until pale golden.

**7.** Cool on a rack.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: bake the batch, cool fully, wrap each one. Warm from frozen in the oven.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen.</span>
