<h1>Pandan Cake</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">65 min bake</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">25cm tube tin</span>

eggs               8 large
cake flour         180g
caster sugar       160g
coconut cream      70ml
pandan powder      1½ tbsp
pandan paste       ½ tsp
water              110ml
vegetable oil      100g
baking powder      1 tsp
cream of tartar    ¾ tsp
salt               ½ tsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Japanese Violet cake flour.<br>Coconut milk instead of cream: 140ml, and cut the water to 40ml.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 150°C. Separate the eggs while cold and leave them out 30 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Room temperature whites whip higher than cold ones.</span>

**2.** In a large bowl, whisk smooth by hand:

{{< ingr "egg yolks=8, caster sugar=40g, coconut cream=70ml, water=110ml, pandan paste=½ tsp, vegetable oil=100g" >}}

**3.** Sift in and whisk to a loose batter:

{{< ingr "cake flour=180g, pandan powder=1½ tbsp, baking powder=1 tsp, salt=½ tsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stop once smooth. Overmixed batter bakes short and dense.</span>

**4.** In the stand mixer with the whisk attachment, on medium, whisk the whites and cream of tartar until foamy, then add the sugar in three goes and whip to stiff peaks:

{{< ingr "egg whites=8, cream of tartar=¾ tsp, caster sugar=120g" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stiff but not dry. Lifted out, the peak should stand up and flop at the very tip.<br>Underwhipped, the cake bakes short and dense. Overwhipped, it clumps and needs more folding, which knocks the air out.</span>

**5.** By hand with a spatula, fold a third of the meringue into the batter to loosen it, then fold in the rest until no white streaks are left.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Every extra fold pops air and the cake sinks.<br>White streaks left in bake into holes.</span>

**6.** Pour into the tin and bake 55 minutes, then turn the oven up to 165°C for 10 minutes more, until a skewer comes out clean. Keep the door shut.

**7.** Invert the tin on its legs the second it comes out. Leave 3 hours until stone cold, then run a knife round the edge and the centre to turn out.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Store: cool completely before covering, then keep sealed at room temperature.<br>Keeps 2 to 3 days, 2 in humid weather. Not the fridge, it dries it out.</span>
