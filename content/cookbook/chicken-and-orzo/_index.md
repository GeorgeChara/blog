<h1>Chicken and Orzo</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One pot, 45 min, 200°C (180°C fan), serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">chicken thighs   8, bone in skin on
orzo             300g
onion            1, chopped
garlic           4 cloves, sliced
tinned tomatoes  1 tin
chicken stock    600ml, hot
dried oregano    1 tsp
ground cinnamon  pinch
feta             100g, to finish
olive oil        2 tbsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Close cousin of <a href="/cookbook/giouvetsi/">giouvetsi</a>. This is the fast weeknight version with chicken instead of beef.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

**2.** Brown the chicken skin side down in the Dutch oven for 5 minutes. Set aside.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The only browning step. Worth it here because the rendered fat cooks the onions.</span>

**3.** Soften the onion and garlic in the fat, then add the rest:

{{< ingr "onion=1 chopped, garlic=4 cloves sliced, tinned tomatoes=1 tin, chicken stock=600ml hot, orzo=300g, dried oregano=1 tsp, ground cinnamon=pinch" >}}

**4.** Chicken back on top, skin side up.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Measure the stock. The orzo needs the full 600ml to cook through, so this is a ratio.<br>The skin should sit proud of the liquid. Submerged skin stews and stays pale.</span>

**5.** Oven, lid off, 30 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stir the orzo once at the halfway point or the edges catch.</span>

**6.** Crumble the feta over before serving.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Eat it the day you make it. Orzo turns to paste on reheating.</span>
