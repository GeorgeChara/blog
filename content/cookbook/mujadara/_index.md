<h1>Mujadara</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Rice, lentils and burnt onions, one pot, 1 hr, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">brown lentils    200g
basmati rice     200g
onions           4, thinly sliced
olive oil        100ml
ground cumin     2 tsp
ground coriander 1 tsp
cinnamon stick   1
water            900ml
salt             to season
yoghurt          to serve
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Same dish as Cypriot moutzentra. Costs about 50p a portion.<br>The onions are not a garnish. They are the entire flavour of the dish, so don't rush them.<br>Vegan if you serve it without the yoghurt.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Fry the onions slowly in the oil for 25 to 30 minutes, until deep brown and sweet. Lift half out onto kitchen paper.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Low and slow, past golden and into properly brown. This is 90% of the work and all of the taste. If they are still only golden at 25 minutes, keep going.</span>

**2.** Add to the pot and simmer 15 minutes:

{{< ingr "brown lentils=200g, water=900ml, cinnamon stick=1" >}}

**3.** Rinse the rice, then stir it in with the spices. Lid on, lowest heat, 15 minutes:

{{< ingr "basmati rice=200g rinsed, ground cumin=2 tsp, ground coriander=1 tsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Top up with a splash of water if it looks dry before the rice is done.</span>

**4.** Off the heat, lid on, rest 10 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The rest is what finishes the rice. Don't lift the lid.</span>

**5.** Fork it through, salt, and pile the reserved onions on top.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Eat with yoghurt and a squeeze of lemon. Good hot or at room temperature.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, freeze in portions for up to 3 months. Keep the crisp onions separate, they go soft.<br>Reheat: from frozen, covered, with a splash of water.</span>
