<h1>Italian Deli Pasta Salad</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">make ahead, serves 4, 20 min</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Salad</span>
pasta              300g
cherry tomatoes    250g
mozzarella         150g
roasted peppers    150g
salami             100g
black olives       100g

<span style="color: #888;">Dressing</span>
olive oil          5 tbsp
red wine vinegar   2 tbsp
Dijon mustard      1 tsp
dried oregano      1 tsp
garlic             1 clove
sugar              pinch
salt and pepper    to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lountza works in place of the salami, fried halloumi in place of the mozzarella. The mustard emulsifies the dressing so it stays loose and clings, even cold from the fridge.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Cook the pasta a touch firmer than al dente, drain, rinse under cold water and toss with a little oil so it doesn't clump.

**2.** Shake the dressing together in a jar until thick and emulsified.

**3.** Combine, then toss through the dressing:

{{< ingr "pasta=300g, tomatoes=250g, mozzarella=150g, peppers=150g, salami=100g, olives=100g" >}}

Fresh basil on top if you have it.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Making it for the week? Hold a little dressing back, pasta drinks it up as it sits. Refresh with a splash of oil and vinegar at lunch.<br>Keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge, airtight (it has dairy and cured meat). Don't freeze. Out on the side: 1 hour max in the heat.</span>
