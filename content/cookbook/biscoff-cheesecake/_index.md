<h1>Biscoff Cheesecake</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">No bake, 4 hr chill, 18cm springform, serves 12</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Base</span>
Biscoff biscuits  200g
unsalted butter    80g

<span style="color: #888;">Filling</span>
double cream      300ml
cream cheese      400g
Biscoff spread    200g

<span style="color: #888;">Topping</span>
Biscoff spread    150g
Biscoff biscuits   50g
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Full fat cream cheese, the low fat tubs will not set.<br>Everything cold from the fridge.<br>250g biscuits and 350g spread in total, so two jars.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Line the bottom of the cake tin with baking paper, so the finished cheesecake slides off the base and into a box.

**2.** Crush the biscuits to fine crumbs and stir through the melted butter. Press into the bottom of the cake tin to form the base and put in the fridge.

**3.** Whisk together until very thick:

{{< ingr "double cream=300ml, cream cheese=400g, Biscoff spread=200g" >}}

**4.** Spoon onto the base and smooth the top. Cover and chill at least 3 hr, overnight is better.

**5.** Melt the remaining spread, 20 seconds at a time, until it pours. Pour over and spread to the edges. Scatter the last of the biscuits round the rim. Put in the fridge for an hour.

**6.** Run a warm knife round the inside of the tin before unclipping it.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Keeps 3 days in the fridge, best topped on the day.<br>Freezes undecorated. Defrost 24 hours in the fridge, still in the tin, then top.</span>
