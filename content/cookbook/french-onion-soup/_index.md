<h1>French Onion Soup</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">1 hr 15 min, Dutch oven, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Soup</span>
onions             1kg
butter             50g
olive oil          1 tbsp
garlic             3 cloves
plain flour        1 tbsp
white wine         150ml
beef stock         1.2L
bay leaves         2
fresh thyme        few sprigs
salt and pepper    to taste

<span style="color: #888;">Topping</span>
<a href="/cookbook/baguette/">baguette</a>           1/2
gruyère            150g
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The caramelising is the whole dish, it takes patience not skill. Don't rush it or the soup will be pale and thin.<br>A pinch of sugar with the onions speeds up the caramelising, cutting it to about 25 minutes.<br>Gruyère is a Swiss cheese. Any Swiss cheese works in a pinch. A splash of brandy with the wine adds depth.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Halve and thinly slice the onions.

**2.** Melt the butter with the oil in the Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onions and a pinch of salt. Cook for 35/45 minutes, stirring often, until deep golden brown and jammy. This is the key step.

**3.** Add the garlic and cook 1 minute. Stir in the flour and cook 1 minute more.

**4.** Pour in the wine, scraping the base of the pot, and let it bubble for 2 minutes.

**5.** Add the stock, bay leaves and thyme. Simmer for 20/30 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.

**6.** Toast slices of baguette and rub each one with a cut garlic clove while still warm.

**7.** Ladle the soup into ovenproof bowls, float a slice or two of toast on each and pile with grated gruyère.

**8.** Grill until the cheese is melted, bubbling and golden.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:-0.5em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">For a richer top, spread the baguette with garlic butter before toasting instead of rubbing with raw garlic.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">No ovenproof bowls: grill the cheese on the toast separately, then float on top.</span>
