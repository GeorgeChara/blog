<h1>Pot Roast Chicken</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One pot, 1 hr 20, 200°C (180°C fan), serves 4 with leftovers</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">chicken          1.6kg, whole
potatoes         1kg, halved
carrots          4, in thick chunks
onions           2, in wedges
garlic           1 bulb, cloves separated, skins on
lemon            1, halved
thyme            few sprigs
olive oil        3 tbsp
salt and pepper  to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Cut the veg bigger than feels right. Small pieces dissolve before the bird is done.<br>Parsnips, shallots and squash all work. Green veg doesn't, it turns to sludge over an hour. Do that on the side.<br>Day one of three. The leftovers and the carcass are two more dinners.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Get the chicken out of the fridge half an hour before, if you remember.

**2.** Into the Dutch oven with the oil. Toss:

{{< ingr "potatoes=1kg halved, carrots=4 in thick chunks, onions=2 in wedges, garlic=1 bulb skins on, olive oil=3 tbsp" >}}

**3.** Half the lemon and the thyme into the cavity. Sit the chicken on top of the veg. Salt the skin hard.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The veg is the roasting rack. Everything that renders off the bird drips into it, and that is the entire reason to do this in the Dutch oven rather than a tray.</span>

**4.** Lid on, 45 minutes.

**5.** Lid off, 20 minutes more, until the skin is properly brown.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lid off at the end is the whole trick. Steam is what stops skin crisping, so it has to escape.</span>

**6.** Check the thickest part of the thigh is 75°C. Rest 15 minutes in the pot, lid off.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lid on while it rests and the trapped steam softens the skin you just spent 20 minutes on.</span>

**7.** Toss the veg through the juices in the bottom and squeeze the other lemon half over.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Carve every last scrap off the bird before you do anything else, and keep the carcass. Meat left on the bones through a three hour stock tastes of nothing afterwards.<br>A 1.6kg bird gives about 500g of picked meat and 1.5 litres of stock off the frame. Two of you eating a roast gets through half of that, so the meat is what runs out, not the stock. Buy a 2kg bird if you want all three meals comfortably.</span>
