<h1>Rice</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Dutch oven, 180°C fan, 45 min, serves 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Serves 6</span>

long grain rice  400g
boiling water    750ml (700ml if rinsed)
salt             1 tsp
butter           knob
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The ratio is 1 mug rice to 1.5 mugs water, by volume. Rinsed rice holds water from the sieve, so drop it to 700ml.<br>Don't go under 400g, small batches crisp at the edges in the oven.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 180°C fan.

**2.** Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs clear, or skip it. Rinsed cooks up separate and fluffy, unrinsed a little stickier.

**3.** Into the cold Dutch oven:

{{< ingr "long grain rice=400g, salt=1 tsp, butter=knob" >}}

**4.** Boil the kettle, measure the water in a jug and pour it over the rice. Stir once.

**5.** Lid on, into the oven for 35 minutes. No peeking.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The lid needs to seal. If steam escapes round the edge, put a sheet of foil under it.</span>

**6.** Out of the oven, lid still on, rest 10 minutes. Fork through before serving.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: cool fast, within an hour, then freeze flat in bags.<br>Reheat: from frozen, piping hot, microwave or a pan with a splash of water. Once only.<br>Keeps: 1 month frozen, 1 day in the fridge.</span>
