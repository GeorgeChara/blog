<h1>Smash Burgers</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">15 min, makes 2 doubles</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">beef mince     500g
cheese slices  4
burger buns    2
salt           to taste
black pepper   to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">20% fat mince, lean won't crust.<br>American or burger cheese melts best.<br>Toasted <a href="/cookbook/brioche-buns/">brioche buns</a> are the one.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Roll the mince into 4 loose balls. Don't pack them tight and don't season yet.

**2.** Get the cast iron skillet ripping hot, no oil.

**3.** Ball into the pan, smash flat within 10 seconds, as thin as you can. Press through a square of baking paper so the spatula doesn't stick. Salt and pepper.

**4.** Don't touch it. 2 to 3 minutes until the edges are brown and lacy.

**5.** Flip, cheese on, cook 1 minute to melt. Stack two patties per bun.

**6.** Toast the buns cut side down in the pan.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Smash fast, while the fat is still cold, or it won't press thin.<br>Oklahoma style: pile thin sliced onion on the raw patty, then flip it onto the onion to cook into the crust.</span>
