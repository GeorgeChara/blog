<h1>Quick Mash</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Microwave mash, no peeling or draining, 20 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">potatoes         1kg, Maris Piper
butter           100g, cubed
salt             to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Maris Piper is the right call. Avoid waxy potatoes like Charlotte or new potatoes.<br>Loosen with a splash of hot water if it is too stiff. Milk is optional, butter is what matters.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Prick the potatoes all over with a fork. Microwave on full for 15 minutes, until a knife goes in with no resistance.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Undercooked means lumpy forever. Go by the knife, not the clock.</span>

**2.** Cut in half and scoop the insides into a bowl. Bin the skins.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Hold it with a fork and scoop with a spoon so you don't burn your hands. A ricer is better still, put the half in cut side down and press, the skin stays behind.</span>

**3.** Mash in the butter while it is still steaming.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Butter goes in hot or it won't melt through properly.</span>

**4.** Salt, taste, salt again.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Mash needs more salt than you think.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Never blend it, you get glue.<br>Microwaving drives water off rather than in, so it comes out drier and fluffier than boiled.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, then freeze in portions.<br>Reheat: from frozen with a knob of butter, low and slow, stirring.</span>
