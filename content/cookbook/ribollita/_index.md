<h1>Ribollita</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Tuscan bean, veg and bread stew, one pot, 1.5 hr, serves 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">The soffritto</span>
onion            1, finely chopped
carrots          2, finely chopped
celery           2 sticks, finely chopped
garlic           3 cloves, sliced
olive oil        4 tbsp

<span style="color: #888;">The stew</span>
cannellini beans 2 tins, drained
chopped tomatoes 1 x 400g tin
potatoes         300g, diced
vegetable stock  1 litre
thyme            few sprigs
rosemary         1 sprig
cavolo nero      200g, shredded
savoy cabbage    200g, shredded
salt and pepper  to season

<span style="color: #888;">To finish</span>
stale bread      200g, torn
olive oil        to serve
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Cavolo nero is the soul of it. Kale stands in if you cannot get it.<br>The stale bread is the point, not a way to use up scraps. A heel of your <a href="/cookbook/sourdough/">sourdough</a> or <a href="/cookbook/boule/">boule</a> is ideal.<br>The name means reboiled. It is meant to be made a day ahead and warmed through again.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Soften the soffritto in the oil in the Dutch oven over low heat, 10 minutes, until sweet and not coloured:

{{< ingr "onion=1 finely chopped, carrots=2 finely chopped, celery=2 sticks, garlic=3 cloves sliced" >}}

**2.** Add and bring to a gentle simmer:

{{< ingr "chopped tomatoes=1 tin, potatoes=300g diced, vegetable stock=1 litre, thyme=few sprigs, rosemary=1 sprig" >}}

**3.** Mash a ladle of the beans to a rough paste, then stir all the beans in. Simmer 20 minutes.

**4.** Stir in the cavolo nero and cabbage. Simmer 30 minutes, until everything is soft and the potatoes are starting to collapse. Season.

**5.** Stir in the torn bread. Cook 15 minutes more, mashing the bread and some of the potato against the side of the pot, until it breaks down and the stew turns thick.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Thick and spoonable, not soupy. The bread does the thickening, so give it the full time to break down. A splash of stock if it goes too stiff.</span>

**6.** Rest off the heat 10 minutes. Serve in bowls with a very generous pour of olive oil over each.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, freeze in portions for up to 3 months. It is stodgier reheated, which is the point of the dish.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a pan, loosen with a splash of stock or water.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Source: <a href="https://www.healthyseasonalrecipes.com/tuscan-ribollita/">Healthy Seasonal Recipes</a>.</span>
