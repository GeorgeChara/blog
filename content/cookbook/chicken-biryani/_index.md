<h1>Chicken Biryani</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Layered rice, one pot, 1 hr 15, serves 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">The marinade</span>
chicken thighs   800g, boneless, in chunks
yoghurt          200g
garlic           6 cloves, crushed
ginger           thumb, grated
garam masala     2 tsp
ground turmeric  1 tsp
ground cumin     2 tsp
chilli powder    1 tsp

<span style="color: #888;">The rice</span>
basmati rice     400g
cinnamon stick   1
cardamom pods    6
bay leaves       2

<span style="color: #888;">To layer</span>
onions           3, thinly sliced
ghee or butter   75g
saffron          pinch, in 3 tbsp warm milk
coriander        handful, chopped
mint             handful, chopped
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The traditional one is goat, or lamb as the usual substitute. Chicken is the modern version, an hour instead of three and a third of the price.<br>The rice is deliberately undercooked before layering. It finishes in the steam.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Mix and leave 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge:

{{< ingr "chicken thighs=800g in chunks, yoghurt=200g, garlic=6 cloves crushed, ginger=thumb grated, garam masala=2 tsp, ground turmeric=1 tsp, ground cumin=2 tsp, chilli powder=1 tsp" >}}

**2.** Fry the onions in the ghee until deep brown, 20 to 25 minutes. Lift two thirds out.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Same rule as mujadara. Brown, not golden.</span>

**3.** Tip the marinated chicken into the pot with the remaining onions. Cook 10 minutes.

**4.** Meanwhile boil the rice in plenty of salted water for 5 minutes only, then drain:

{{< ingr "basmati rice=400g, cinnamon stick=1, cardamom pods=6, bay leaves=2" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Five minutes. It should still have a hard bite. It cooks the rest of the way in step 6.</span>

**5.** Spread the rice over the chicken, then scatter over the top:

{{< ingr "fried onions=two thirds, coriander=handful, mint=handful, saffron milk=3 tbsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Don't stir. The layers are the point.</span>

**6.** Lid on tight, lowest possible heat, 25 minutes. Or 160°C in the oven.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">If the lid is loose, put foil under it. All the steam has to stay in.</span>

**7.** Rest 10 minutes, then dig down and fork it through from the bottom.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, freeze in portions for up to 3 months.<br>Reheat: from frozen, covered, with a splash of water so the rice steams rather than dries.</span>
