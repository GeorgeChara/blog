<h1>Creamy Chicken and Gnocchi</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">serves 3-4, 30 min, one pan</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Chicken</span>
chicken thighs     6, boneless skinless
olive oil          2 tbsp
paprika            1 tsp
salt and pepper    to season

<span style="color: #888;">Sauce</span>
garlic             3 cloves, sliced
sun-dried tomatoes 100g, chopped
white wine         splash, optional
chicken stock      200ml
double cream       200ml
parmesan           40g, grated
italian seasoning  1 tsp

<span style="color: #888;">Finish</span>
gnocchi            500g (1 pack)
spinach            large handful
basil              to serve
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Boneless skinless thighs are easiest here, the skin would soften in the sauce anyway. Use the oil from the sun-dried tomato jar to brown the chicken if you like.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Season the thighs with paprika, salt and pepper. Brown in the oil on medium high, a few minutes each side, then set aside.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">They finish in the sauce, so just get colour now, not cooked through.</span>

**2.** Lower the heat. Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes, cook 1 minute, then splash in the wine and let it bubble away.

**3.** Pour in the stock, cream, parmesan and Italian seasoning. Stir to a smooth sauce.

**4.** Add the gnocchi straight from the pack. Simmer 4 to 5 minutes until tender and the sauce thickens.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The gnocchi cooks right in the sauce, no need to boil it first.</span>

**5.** Slice or leave the chicken whole, return it with any juices, add the spinach, and cook 3 to 4 minutes until cooked through and the spinach wilts.

**6.** Finish with basil and extra parmesan.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Double cream gives the richest sauce. For lighter, use half cream and half stock, it will be thinner.</span>
