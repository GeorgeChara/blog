<h1>Chana Saag</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One pot, 25 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">chickpeas        2 tins, drained
spinach          400g
onion            1, chopped
garlic           4 cloves, chopped
ginger           thumb, grated
tinned tomatoes  1 tin
ground cumin     2 tsp
ground coriander 2 tsp
garam masala     1 tsp
ground turmeric  1 tsp
oil              2 tbsp
lemon            1/2, to finish
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Frozen spinach works and is cheaper. Use 300g and add it with the tomatoes.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Soften in the oil for 5 minutes:

{{< ingr "onion=1 chopped, garlic=4 cloves chopped, ginger=thumb grated, oil=2 tbsp" >}}

**2.** Add the spices and stir for 1 minute:

{{< ingr "ground cumin=2 tsp, ground coriander=2 tsp, garam masala=1 tsp, ground turmeric=1 tsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Frying the spices first. Half a minute in hot oil is what stops them tasting like dust.</span>

**3.** Tomatoes and chickpeas in, plus a splash of water. Simmer 15 minutes:

{{< ingr "tinned tomatoes=1 tin, chickpeas=2 tins drained, water=splash" >}}

**4.** Add the spinach a handful at a time until it wilts down.

**5.** Salt, then a squeeze of lemon.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The lemon at the end is what stops it tasting flat.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, freeze in portions for up to 3 months.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a pan with a splash of water. Fresh lemon at the end again.</span>
