<h1>Skillet Lasagna</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">serves 3-4, 50 min, one pan, no layering</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">olive oil           1 tbsp
beef mince          400g
onion               1 medium, chopped
garlic              2 cloves, crushed
tomato puree        1 tbsp
passata             500g
water               150ml
dried oregano       1 tsp
lasagne sheets      6-8 (approx 150g)
ricotta             250g
mozzarella          125g (1 ball), torn
parmesan            30g, grated
basil               small handful
salt and pepper     to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Italian sausage meat works instead of beef. Shortcut: skip the build and use 500-600g ready ragu loosened with 100-150ml water, then start at step 3.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.5em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The pasta cooks in the sauce and the starch it sheds thickens everything, silkier than oven lasagna in half the time. To stop it going loose, reduce the ragu first and rest it at the end so the starch sets.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oil in a large lidded skillet over medium heat. Brown the mince well, breaking it up, then the onion for 4 to 5 minutes until soft, then the garlic and tomato puree for 1 minute:

{{< ingr "olive oil=1 tbsp, beef mince=400g, onion=1 medium chopped, garlic=2 cloves crushed, tomato puree=1 tbsp" >}}

**2.** Add and simmer uncovered 8 to 10 minutes until thick and clinging, not soupy. Stir in the water, the pasta will drink it:

{{< ingr "passata=500g, dried oregano=1 tsp, salt and pepper=to taste, water=150ml" >}}

**3.** Break the dry lasagne sheets into rough 3-5cm pieces and scatter over the sauce. Push every piece fully under so they cook evenly.

**4.** Cover and simmer gently for about 20 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the pasta is tender. Add a splash of water if it tightens too fast.

**5.** Heat the grill to high. Dot the ricotta over the surface, scatter the rest, and grill 3 to 5 minutes until bubbling and golden in patches:

{{< ingr "ricotta=250g, mozzarella=125g torn, parmesan=30g grated" >}}

**6.** Rest off the heat for 8 to 10 minutes so the starch sets and it slices cleanly. Scatter with basil and serve.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Too loose? reduce the sauce more next time, add a sheet, and rest it longer. Keep the handle out from under the grill or wrap it in foil.</span>
