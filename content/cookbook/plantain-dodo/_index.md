<h1>Plantain (Dodo)</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">fried sweet plantain, 15 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Serves 4</span>

ripe plantain   3
oil             for frying
salt            pinch
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Use ripe plantain, yellow with black spots. The blacker the skin the sweeter it fries. Green plantain stays starchy and won't sweeten.<br>Serve with <a href="/cookbook/jollof-rice/">jollof rice</a>.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Top and tail the plantain, score the skin down its length and peel.

**2.** Slice on the diagonal, about 1cm thick.

**3.** Shallow fry in hot oil for 2/3 minutes each side until deep golden and caramelised at the edges.

**4.** Drain on kitchen paper and sprinkle with a little salt.
