<h1>Pupusas</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Salvadoran cheese flatbreads, 30 min, makes 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Makes 6</span>

masa harina   250g
warm water    300ml
salt          1/2 tsp
mozzarella    150g
oil           for cooking
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Masa harina only, not cornflour or polenta. Latin and Mexican shops stock it, or online. This is corn dough, not the same as <a href="/cookbook/flour-tortillas/">flour tortillas</a>.<br>Mozzarella melts and stretches without bursting through. Quesillo is the authentic cheese if you can get it.<br>Serve with salsa and <a href="/cookbook/lime-cabbage-slaw/">slaw</a> (curtido).</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Mix into a soft, smooth, moist dough:

{{< ingr "masa harina=250g, salt=1/2 tsp, warm water=300ml" >}}

Rest 10 minutes. If it cracks when pressed, work in a splash more water.

**2.** Divide into 6 balls. Press a deep well into each, pack in cheese, then pinch the dough closed over the top.

**3.** Gently flatten each into a disc about 10cm wide, patching any cracks with damp fingers.

**4.** Cook on a lightly oiled pan over medium heat for 3 minutes a side, until golden with brown spots and the cheese is molten.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: shape the filled pupusas, freeze raw on a tray then bag once solid.<br>Reheat: cook from frozen on a lightly oiled pan, a couple of minutes longer per side.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen.</span>
