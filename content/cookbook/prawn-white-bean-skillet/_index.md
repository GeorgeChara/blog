<h1>Prawn, White Bean and Feta Skillet</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">serves 2-3, 20 min, one pan</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">olive oil         2 tbsp
onion             1 small, sliced
garlic            2 cloves, sliced
dried oregano     1 tsp
chopped tomatoes  400g tin
prawns            250g, raw peeled
cannellini beans  400g tin, drained
spinach           2 handfuls
feta              50g, crumbled
salt and pepper   to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">One pan, under 20 minutes. Good with crusty bread to mop the sauce.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oil in the pan over medium-high. Soften the onion, 4 to 5 minutes, then add the garlic and oregano and cook 1 minute.

**2.** Pour in the tomatoes, season, and simmer until slightly thickened, 2 to 3 minutes.

**3.** Add the prawns and beans. Cook 2 to 3 minutes until the prawns turn pink.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Raw prawns cook fast. Pull them the moment they turn pink or they go rubbery.</span>

**4.** Stir in the spinach and cook until just wilted and the prawns are cooked through, 2 to 3 minutes.

**5.** Off the heat, scatter over the feta and cover for 2 to 3 minutes to warm through, then serve.
