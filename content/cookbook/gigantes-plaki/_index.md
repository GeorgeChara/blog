<h1>Gigantes Plaki</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Greek baked butter beans, vegan, one pot, 50 min, 170°C, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">The beans</span>
butter beans     2 tins, drained

<span style="color: #888;">The sauce</span>
onion            1, finely chopped
carrot           1, finely chopped
celery           1 stick, finely chopped
garlic           2 cloves, sliced
chopped tomatoes 1 x 400g tin
tomato puree     1 tbsp
olive oil        100ml
dried oregano    1 tsp
ground cinnamon  pinch
bay leaf         1
parsley          handful, chopped
salt and pepper  to season

<span style="color: #888;">To finish</span>
halloumi         200g, sliced (optional)
feta             optional
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The generous olive oil is the dish, not a lot to cut back. This is ladera, cooked in oil.<br>Dried gigantes soaked overnight and simmered until tender are the real giant beans. Tinned butter beans are the weeknight route.<br>Leave the feta off to keep it vegan.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 170°C. Soften in the oil in an ovenproof pan over medium heat, 8 minutes:

{{< ingr "onion=1 finely chopped, carrot=1 finely chopped, celery=1 stick, garlic=2 cloves sliced" >}}

**2.** Stir in the tomato puree for a minute, then add and simmer 5 minutes:

{{< ingr "chopped tomatoes=1 tin, dried oregano=1 tsp, ground cinnamon=pinch, bay leaf=1" >}}

**3.** Stir in the beans and most of the parsley. Season. Add a splash of water so the beans sit just covered in a loose sauce.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">It should look a little too saucy going in. The beans drink it and the top sets in the oven.</span>

**4.** Into the oven, uncovered, 30 to 40 minutes, until the top is crusty and the sauce has thickened around the beans.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">To level it up with halloumi, lay the slices on top for the last 20 minutes so they bake golden. Or fry them in a dry pan until crisp and lay them on at the end. Skip the feta if you do, and hold the salt back, halloumi brings plenty.</span>

**5.** Rest 10 minutes. Drizzle with olive oil, scatter the rest of the parsley, and crumble over feta if using. Serve warm or at room temperature with bread.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, freeze in portions for up to 3 months. Add the feta fresh.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a low oven or a pan until piping hot.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Sources: <a href="https://www.themediterraneandish.com/gigantes-plaki-greek-giant-beans/">The Mediterranean Dish</a>, <a href="https://realgreekrecipes.com/butter-beans-recipe/">Real Greek Recipes</a>.</span>
