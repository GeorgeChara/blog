<h1>Tomato Soup</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">50 min, Dutch oven, serves 4</p>

<img loading="lazy" src="/images/tomato-soup/header.webp" alt="Tomato Soup">

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">tinned tomatoes    1.6kg
butter             115g
onion              1
garlic             4 cloves
salt               to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">San Marzano if you can get them, otherwise good plum tomatoes. For a blended soup like this, 1 tin San Marzano and 3 of good plum is plenty.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Quarter the onion and smash the garlic. Everything into the Dutch oven:

{{< ingr "tinned tomatoes=1.6kg, butter=115g, onion=1 quartered, garlic=4 cloves smashed" >}}

**2.** Bring to a gentle simmer over low heat, lid half on.

**3.** Simmer 45 minutes. Stir every 15 minutes so nothing catches on the base.

**4.** Remove the onion quarters and leave the garlic. Blend smooth with a stick blender. The butter emulsifies into the tomatoes and turns it velvety.

**5.** Now season. Add salt a little at a time, stirring and tasting as you go. Salting here, not at the start, is the trick: the soup reduces as it simmers, so seasoning against the finished volume stops it coming out too salty.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Serve with a <a href="/cookbook/cheese-toastie/">cheese toastie</a>.</span>
