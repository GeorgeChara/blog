<h1>Matilda Chocolate Cake</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">1 hr plus chilling, 160°C, three 20cm tins</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Cake</span>
plain flour          260g
baking powder        1.5 tsp
bicarbonate of soda  1.5 tsp
caster sugar         200g
light brown sugar    135g
salt                 1 tsp
eggs                 3
vegetable oil        135g
buttermilk           80g
soured cream         240g
Dutch cocoa          75g
instant coffee       2 tsp
boiling water        180g

<span style="color: #888;">Fudge frosting</span>
dark chocolate       300g, 70%
golden syrup         3 tbsp
cream cheese         225g
cocoa powder         33g
icing sugar          100g
salt                 1/4 tsp
double cream         300ml, chilled
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Weigh the flour, too much makes it dry. Dutch cocoa and 70% dark chocolate (bars, not chips) give the deepest colour and the glossy fudge shine.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 160°C. Grease and line three 20cm tins.

**2.** Whisk the dry in one bowl:

{{< ingr "plain flour=260g, baking powder=1.5 tsp, bicarbonate of soda=1.5 tsp, caster sugar=200g, light brown sugar=135g, salt=1 tsp" >}}

In another, whisk the wet until smooth:

{{< ingr "eggs=3, vegetable oil=135g, buttermilk=80g, soured cream=240g" >}}

**3.** Mix the cocoa and coffee into the boiling water to a smooth paste.

**4.** Fold the dry mix into the wet in two goes, then fold in the cocoa paste.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stop the moment it comes together. Overmixing makes it tough.</span>

**5.** Divide between the tins and bake 22 to 24 minutes, until a skewer comes out with moist crumbs.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Pull it at moist crumbs, not clean. An overbaked chocolate cake goes dry.</span>

**6.** Cool in the tins 20 minutes, turn out, then chill 1 hour.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Cold layers are firmer and far easier to frost neatly.</span>

**7.** Melt the chocolate with the golden syrup over low heat, stirring, then set aside.

**8.** In the stand mixer, beat until creamy:

{{< ingr "cream cheese=225g, cocoa powder=33g, icing sugar=100g, salt=1/4 tsp" >}}

Add the cream and whisk to fluffy, then pour in the warm chocolate and whisk to a glossy, spreadable fudge.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">If it firms up too much, warm a spoonful and beat it back through.</span>

**9.** Stack the layers with frosting between, then coat the top and sides in a thick, glossy layer.
