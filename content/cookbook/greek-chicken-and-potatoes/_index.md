<h1>Greek Chicken and Potatoes</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One tray, 1 hr 20, 200°C (180°C fan), serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">chicken thighs   8, bone in skin on
potatoes         1.2kg, in thick wedges
lemons           2, juice
garlic           6 cloves, crushed
dried oregano    2 tbsp
olive oil        100ml
chicken stock    250ml
salt and pepper  to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Skin on the potatoes. They go soft underneath and crisp at the edges.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

**2.** Into the dish and toss it all together:

{{< ingr "potatoes=1.2kg in wedges, garlic=6 cloves crushed, dried oregano=2 tbsp, olive oil=100ml, lemons=2 juiced, chicken stock=250ml" >}}

**3.** Sit the chicken on top, skin side up. Salt the skin hard.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The chicken fat renders down into the potatoes. That is the whole dish.<br>The 250ml is a guide, not a ratio. The liquid should come roughly halfway up the potatoes and stay below the skin. Too deep and you boil the potatoes instead of roasting them.<br>Top up with a splash of hot water at the halfway baste if the tray is running dry.</span>

**4.** Oven, no lid, 1 hour 15 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">No lid at all. Anything covered steams and you get pale skin and wet potatoes.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The long bake is for the potatoes, not the chicken. Thighs hit 75°C in about 45 minutes, so they finish well done rather than juicy. That is how this dish works, the fat renders out into the potatoes.<br>Want the chicken juicier: give the potatoes 30 minutes on their own first, then sit the chicken on and roast 45.<br>Thighs take it, breast does not. Don't swap.</span>

**5.** Baste the chicken once halfway through.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Serve straight from the dish with the lemony juices spooned over.</span>
