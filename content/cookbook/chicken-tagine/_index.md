<h1>Chicken Tagine</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">with preserved lemon and olives, 1 hr 30 min, Dutch oven, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Chicken</span>
chicken thighs     8
olive oil          for browning
salt and pepper    to season

<span style="color: #888;">Base</span>
onion              2
garlic             4 cloves
ginger             1 inch
ground cumin       2 tsp
paprika            1 tsp
ground cinnamon    1/2 tsp
ground turmeric    1 tsp
saffron            pinch, optional
chicken stock      300ml

<span style="color: #888;">To finish</span>
preserved lemon    1
green olives       100g
fresh coriander    handful
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Bone-in thighs give the most flavour, skin on or off. No tagine needed, the Dutch oven does the same job.<br>The <a href="/cookbook/preserved-lemons/">preserved lemon</a> goes in near the end so it perfumes the sauce without falling apart. It is salty, so go easy on added salt.<br>A teaspoon of ras el hanout can stand in for the individual spices.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper. Brown all over in olive oil in the Dutch oven, then set aside.

**2.** Soften the sliced onions and garlic in the same pot for 8 minutes. Add the grated ginger and all the spices and cook 1/2 minutes until fragrant.

**3.** Return the chicken. Add the stock and the saffron if using. Bring to a simmer, lid on, and cook low for 45 minutes until the chicken is tender.

**4.** Quarter the preserved lemon, scrape out the pulp and slice the rind. Add it with the olives. Lid off, and cook a further 15 minutes to reduce the sauce and let the lemon infuse.

**5.** Finish with torn coriander. Serve with couscous or rice.
