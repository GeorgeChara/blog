<h1>Flour Tortillas</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">25 min, pan sized, makes 8</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Makes 8 large</span>

plain flour   450g
lard/trex     90g
warm water    240g
salt          8g
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Four ingredients, no baking powder. Leavening puffs them up bread-like, which is the opposite of the thin stretchy tortilla you want. The authentic Sonoran style uses none.<br>Lard is traditional and gives the best result. No lard: vegetable shortening (Trex) is the closest match, butter works but turns them more pita-like, oil is the weakest option.<br>Trex is in the baking aisle of most UK supermarkets, a white block in red packaging, stored at room temperature not chilled. It is vegetarian and vegan, so a good lard alternative for a meat-free version.<br>Water should be warm, around 50°C. Helps the fat incorporate cleanly.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Mix the flour and salt. Add the lard and rub in with your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs with no large lumps.

**2.** Add the warm water gradually, mixing as you go, until a shaggy dough forms. Knead for 5 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest for 25 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:-0.5em; margin-bottom:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stand mixer: paddle attachment to rub the lard into the flour on speed 1, then swap to the dough hook, add the water and knead on speed 2 for 3/4 minutes until smooth.</span>

**3.** Divide into 8 equal pieces. Roll each into a ball, cover and rest a further 10 minutes.

**4.** On a lightly floured surface, roll each ball out paper thin, aiming for 25cm. This is the make or break step. The dough should be thin enough to see the shadow of your hand through it. Too thick and they puff up and eat like pita.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:-0.5em; margin-bottom:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Roll from the centre outward, rotating the dough rather than the rolling pin. If it springs back, leave it 2/3 minutes before continuing. Flour both sides lightly to stop it sticking, but keep it light as excess flour toughens them.</span>

**5.** Heat a dry pan over medium-high heat. No oil. Cook each tortilla 30/45 seconds per side until brown spots appear. Keep it quick, overcooking dries them out and makes them stiff.

**6.** Wrap in a clean tea towel as they come out to keep them warm and pliable. Serve immediately.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to a week. Reheat dry in the pan for 20 seconds per side.</span>
