<h1>Cheesesteak</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">15 min, makes 2</p>

Thin beef and melted cheese on a soft roll. 90% of a real Philly from a UK supermarket. Two things make or break it: the bread and how thin the beef is. Get those right and the cheese barely matters.

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">beef sirloin       340g
onion              1 medium
provolone          120g
soft sub rolls     2
beef dripping      15g
salt               to taste
black pepper       to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Ribeye if you can get it, the marbling is the point. Sirloin is the best value from a supermarket. Skip pre-diced stir fry beef, wrong cut, chewy.<br>Provolone first choice. No provolone? A loose mild cheese sauce beats cheddar, which splits and overpowers.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Freeze the beef 45 to 60 minutes until firm but not solid. Slice as thin as you can against the grain, then chop the slices into rough 1 to 2cm shreds.

**2.** Soften the onions low and slow. Griddle or heavy pan, dripping on medium-low, sliced onions in, 10 minutes until soft and golden. Don't rush it, the sweetness balances the beef. Push aside or remove.

**3.** Crank the heat to high. Beef in, don't crowd the pan. It releases water, let it boil off, then it sears. 3 to 4 minutes, chopping and folding it with the spatula as it cooks.

**4.** Season with salt and pepper, nothing else. No garlic, no herbs.

**5.** Recombine the onions and beef into a pile and lower the heat. Cheese over the top, a splash of water in the pan, lid on for 30 seconds until melted. No lid? Fold an open roll face-down over the meat to trap the heat.

**6.** Scoop the lot, cheese-side, into the warmed roll in one motion.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Warm the rolls first, 3 minutes at 160°C. A cold roll goes soggy. Soft white sub or bakery baguette, avoid anything with a shattering crust, you want yield not crunch.<br>The bread is the real gap. No UK supermarket sells the soft Amoroso roll the original uses. A warmed soft baguette gets you most of the way.<br>Wit or witout: wit is with onions, the default.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;"><strong>Cosmic twist (optional)</strong><br>Same treatment as the souvla. Toss the shredded beef in 100g buttermilk with a pinch of salt and baking powder, 30 to 60 minutes only, no longer or it goes mushy. Drain, pat dry, salt and rest uncovered in the fridge while the onions cook. A deeper, more lacquered crust. Not traditional Philly, a house deviation.</span>
