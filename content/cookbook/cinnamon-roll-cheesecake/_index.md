<h1>Cinnamon Roll Cheesecake</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">70 min bake, 140°C, water bath, 23cm springform, serves 12</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Base</span>
digestive biscuits  180g
brown sugar          55g
cinnamon            1/2 tsp
unsalted butter      85g

<span style="color: #888;">Cinnamon swirl</span>
brown sugar         220g
plain flour          40g
cinnamon            1 tbsp
unsalted butter      75g

<span style="color: #888;">Filling</span>
cream cheese        900g
brown sugar         220g
caster sugar         50g
sour cream          120g
eggs                  4
vanilla             1 tbsp
salt                1/2 tsp

<span style="color: #888;">Frosting</span>
cream cheese         85g
icing sugar         125g
vanilla             1 tsp
double cream        240ml
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Full fat cream cheese and sour cream, the low fat ones will not set.<br>Cream cheese and eggs at room temperature or the batter goes lumpy.<br>Nearly a kilo of cream cheese in the filling, so five tubs in total.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 140°C. Line the bottom of the tin with baking paper and grease the sides.

**2.** Blitz the biscuits to fine crumbs and stir together:

{{< ingr "digestive biscuits=180g, brown sugar=55g, cinnamon=1/2 tsp, unsalted butter=85g melted" >}}

**3.** Press into the bottom of the tin to form the base. Bake 10 min and leave to cool.

**4.** Stir the swirl together and set aside:

{{< ingr "brown sugar=220g, plain flour=40g, cinnamon=1 tbsp, unsalted butter=75g melted" >}}

**5.** Beat the cream cheese 3 min until smooth, then add the sugars and beat 2 min more. Scrape the bowl down:

{{< ingr "cream cheese=900g, brown sugar=220g, caster sugar=50g" >}}

**6.** Beat in the sour cream, then the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla and salt. Stop as soon as it comes together:

{{< ingr "sour cream=120g, eggs=4, vanilla=1 tbsp, salt=1/2 tsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">KitchenAid: paddle attachment, medium speed throughout. Air beaten into the batter is what cracks it.</span>

**7.** Pour a third of the batter over the base and scatter a third of the swirl over. Repeat twice, finishing with swirl, and spread level.

**8.** Wrap the outside of the tin in foil, stand it in a roasting tin and pour hot water halfway up the sides. Bake 60 to 75 min until the edges are set and the centre still wobbles.

**9.** Turn the oven off and leave it inside for an hour. Then chill at least 6 hr, overnight is better.

**10.** Beat the frosting cream cheese with the icing sugar and vanilla. Whip the cream to stiff peaks separately and fold it through:

{{< ingr "cream cheese=85g, icing sugar=125g, vanilla=1 tsp, double cream=240ml" >}}

**11.** Pipe onto the chilled cheesecake and dust with cinnamon.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Keeps 5 days in the fridge in an airtight box.<br>Freezes 2 months undecorated. Defrost overnight in the fridge, then frost.</span>
