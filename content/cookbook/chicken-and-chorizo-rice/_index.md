<h1>Chicken and Chorizo Rice</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One pot, oven, 50 min, 200°C (180°C fan), serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">chicken thighs   8, bone in skin on
chorizo          200g, sliced
long grain rice  300g
onion            1, chopped
garlic           4 cloves, sliced
red pepper       1, sliced
smoked paprika   2 tsp
tomato puree     2 tbsp
chicken stock    700ml, hot
olive oil        2 tbsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Everything goes in raw except the stock, which needs to be hot or the rice cooks unevenly.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

**2.** Everything except the chicken into the Dutch oven. Stir once:

{{< ingr "chorizo=200g sliced, long grain rice=300g, onion=1 chopped, garlic=4 cloves sliced, red pepper=1 sliced, smoked paprika=2 tsp, tomato puree=2 tbsp, chicken stock=700ml hot, olive oil=2 tbsp" >}}

**3.** Sit the chicken on top, skin side up, and salt the skin well.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Measure the stock, don't eyeball it. The rice needs the full 700ml to cook through, so this is a ratio, not a top up.<br>Then check the skin sits proud of the liquid before it goes in the oven. Submerged skin stews and stays pale.<br>If the stock comes up over the skin, your pot is too narrow. Move to a wider one rather than pouring any out.</span>

**4.** Lid on, 25 minutes.

**5.** Lid off, 20 minutes more, until the skin is dark and the rice has drunk the stock.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lid off is what crisps the skin and dries the rice out.</span>

**6.** Rest 5 minutes before serving.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Doesn't freeze well. Reheated rice goes chalky.</span>
