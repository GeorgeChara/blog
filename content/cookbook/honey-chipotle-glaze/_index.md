<h1>Honey Chipotle Glaze</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">5 min, for chicken tenders, coats 500g</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">honey            200g
ketchup          3 tbsp
water            3 tbsp
red wine vinegar 1 tbsp
chipotle paste   1 tsp
salt             pinch
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Chipotle pastes vary in strength, taste and add more.<br>Keeps a month in the fridge.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Put everything in a small pan:

{{< ingr "honey=200g, ketchup=3 tbsp, water=3 tbsp, red wine vinegar=1 tbsp, chipotle paste=1 tsp, salt=pinch" >}}

**2.** Bring to a simmer and cook 2 minutes, stirring. It should coat the back of a spoon and run off slowly. Take it off the heat.

**3.** Toss the tenders in the warm glaze the moment they come out of the oven or fryer.
