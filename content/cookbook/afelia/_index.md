<h1>Afelia</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Cypriot pork in red wine and coriander, marinate then braise, serves 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">The marinade</span>
pork shoulder    1kg, in bite-size pieces
red wine         500ml
coriander seeds  2 tbsp, crushed
cumin            1/2 tsp
bay leaves       2
black pepper     plenty

<span style="color: #888;">To cook</span>
olive oil        80ml
water            120ml
salt             1 tbsp
mushrooms        300g, sliced (optional)
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Coriander seed is the whole point of afelia. Crush it coarsely, do not grind it to powder, so you get little bursts of it.<br>Pork neck or shoulder. The wine both flavours and tenderises, so give it overnight if you can.<br>The mushrooms are optional and not traditional, but they soak up the sauce well.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Mix the pork with the wine, crushed coriander, cumin, bay, and a good grind of pepper. Cover and chill overnight, or at least 3 hours. Drain, keeping the marinade.

**2.** Warm the oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat and brown the pork on all sides.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Pat the pieces dry first so they colour rather than stew in the marinade clinging to them.</span>

**3.** Pour in the reserved marinade and let it bubble, then add the water and salt. Lift out the bay leaves after 10 minutes.

**4.** Lid on, turn down low, and simmer gently for about 1 hour 15 minutes, until the pork is tender and the sauce is dark and reduced. Add a splash of water if it gets too dry.

**5.** If using mushrooms, fry them in a little oil in a separate pan until golden, season, then stir them through the pork for the last couple of minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: cool completely, then portion into bags or tubs (before adding mushrooms).<br>Reheat: from frozen on a low heat with the lid on, or thaw in the fridge first.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen, 3 days in the fridge.</span>
