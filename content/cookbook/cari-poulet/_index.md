<h1>Cari Poulet</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Mauritian chicken curry, 1 hr, Dutch oven, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Serves 4</span>

chicken thighs     1kg
potatoes           500g
onion              1
garlic             4 cloves
ginger             1 inch
tomato             1
curry powder       3 tbsp
ground turmeric    1 tsp
fresh thyme        1 sprig
curry leaves       8
red chillies       2
fresh coriander    handful
oil                for frying
water              400ml
salt               to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Mauritian roasted curry powder is the real thing, sold online. A good Madras curry powder works in a pinch.<br>Thyme and curry leaves are the Mauritian signature. Don't leave them out.<br>The potatoes go fondant in the sauce. Serve with <a href="/cookbook/farata/">farata</a> to scoop it up.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Cut each chicken thigh into 2 or 3 pieces. Peel and dice the potatoes into large chunks.

**2.** Mix the curry powder and turmeric with a splash of water to a loose paste.

**3.** Heat oil in the Dutch oven and soften for 5 minutes:

{{< ingr "onion=1 diced, garlic=4 cloves, ginger=1 inch, fresh thyme=1 sprig, curry leaves=8, red chillies=2 whole" >}}

**4.** Add the curry paste and cook 2/3 minutes until fragrant and the oil splits.

**5.** Add the chicken and turn to coat in the masala. Add the diced tomato and cook down for 5 minutes.

**6.** Bring to a simmer, lid on, and cook 30/40 minutes until the chicken is tender and the potatoes are soft:

{{< ingr "potatoes=500g, water=400ml, salt=to taste" >}}

**7.** Finish with torn coriander. Serve with <a href="/cookbook/farata/">farata</a>.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Vegetarian (cari gros pois): swap the chicken for 2 tins of drained butter beans, the traditional Mauritian version. Add them with the potatoes and use a little less water as they need less cooking. Soya chunks (rehydrated first) or chickpeas work the same way.</span>
