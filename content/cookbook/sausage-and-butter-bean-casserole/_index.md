<h1>Sausage and Butter Bean Casserole</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One pot, no browning, 50 min, 200°C (180°C fan), serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">sausages         8, good ones
butter beans     2 tins, drained
tinned tomatoes  1 tin
onion            1, sliced
garlic           3 cloves
red pepper       1, sliced
smoked paprika   2 tsp
chicken stock    200ml
olive oil        2 tbsp
salt and pepper  to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The laziest thing in the book. Everything goes in raw.<br>Swap the butter beans for cannellini or haricot. Chorizo alongside the sausages if you want it smokier.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).

**2.** Everything into the Dutch oven. Stir once:

{{< ingr "sausages=8, butter beans=2 tins drained, tinned tomatoes=1 tin, onion=1 sliced, garlic=3 cloves, red pepper=1 sliced, smoked paprika=2 tsp, chicken stock=200ml, olive oil=2 tbsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">No browning, no softening. The oven does all of it.</span>

**3.** Lid off, 45 minutes, until the sausages are dark on top and the sauce has thickened.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lid off is important. It needs to reduce, not steam.</span>

**4.** Salt and pepper.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Eat with bread.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, 3 months. The sausages soften on defrosting.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a low oven or a pan, until piping hot through.</span>
