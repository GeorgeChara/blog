<h1>Saganaki</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">serves 2 as a starter, 10 min</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">frying cheese       200g, one slab
plain flour         50g, to dredge
olive oil           3 tbsp, to fry
lemon               1/2
ouzo                1 tbsp, optional
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Use a firm frying cheese: kefalotyri, graviera or kefalograviera. Halloumi works too.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.5em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The dish is named after the pan. A "sagani" is the small two handled frying pan, saganaki is its diminutive. The flour dredge gives a crust that holds the cheese together before it melts through.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Cut the cheese into a slab about 1.5cm thick.

**2.** Dip it briefly in cold water so the surface is wet.

**3.** Dredge in flour, pressing it on, then shake off the excess.

**4.** Heat the oil in a small cast iron skillet over medium high until shimmering.

**5.** Fry the cheese a couple of minutes a side until a deep golden crust forms, flipping carefully. The centre should be soft, not fried through.

**6.** Squeeze over the lemon and serve immediately, in the pan, with crusty bread.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">For the flame: off the heat, pour over a splash of ouzo, light it carefully, then put it out with a squeeze of lemon. Mind your eyebrows.</span>
