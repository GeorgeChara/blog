<h1>Breakfast Burritos</h1>

![](/images/breakfast-burritos/header.webp)

Roll breakfast fillings into <a href="/cookbook/flour-tortillas/">flour tortillas</a>, wrap and freeze. No fixed recipe, pick whatever works from the list.

## Fillings that work

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Carbs</span>
hash brown
potato
beans

<span style="color: #888;">Protein</span>
egg
bacon
sausage
chorizo
black pudding
halloumi

<span style="color: #888;">Veg</span>
mushroom
spinach
roast pepper
caramelised onion
jalapeño

<span style="color: #888;">Cheese</span>
cheddar
feta

<span style="color: #888;">Sauce</span>
ketchup

chipotle tomato
harissa
salsa verde
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">A couple that work: Full English (bacon, sausage, egg, beans, black pudding, <a href="/cookbook/hash-browns/">hash brown</a>, brown sauce), or just egg, cheese, bacon and a hash brown.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Dry fillings only. Cook mushrooms hard, squeeze spinach, cool everything before rolling. Beans stop it going soggy. Sauce in as a thin smear, fresh and dairy at the table.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: cool the fillings, roll into tortillas, wrap each in baking paper, freeze flat.<br>Reheat: from frozen, no need to thaw. Oven is most reliable: wrap in foil, 180°C for 25/30 minutes, then a minute in a dry pan to crisp. Or 90 seconds in the microwave if you are in a rush.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen.</span>
