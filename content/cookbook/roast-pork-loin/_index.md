<h1>Roast Pork Loin</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">with crackling, 240°C (220°C fan) then 180°C (160°C fan), serves 4/6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">pork loin joint   1.5kg
oil               1 tbsp
flaky salt        2 tsp
pepper            to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Loin is lean. Roast to a temperature, never slow-cook it or it dries out.<br>Leave the string on to roast, snip off before carving. Serve with <a href="/cookbook/apple-sauce/">apple sauce</a>, roast potatoes and greens.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 240°C (220°C fan). Pat the rind bone dry. Score the fat 1cm apart if it isn't already, not into the meat. Rub with oil, then salt, into the scores.

**2.** Rind up in a roasting tin. Blast at 240°C (220°C fan) for 25 minutes to start the crackling.

**3.** Drop to 180°C (160°C fan), roast about 30 minutes per 500g, until a thermometer reads 65°C. Go by the thermometer.

**4.** Rest under loose foil 15 minutes, snip the string, carve.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Best crackling: dry the rind uncovered in the fridge overnight first. A kettle of boiling water over the scores helps too.<br>From frozen: fridge the night before, next day score and leave uncovered overnight (thaws and dries in one), roast the day after. Oil and salt only on the day.<br>Gravy: roast on a bed of halved onions and garlic, use the pan juices.<br>Porchetta style: untie, rub inside with garlic, fennel, rosemary and lemon zest, reroll and tie.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: leftover slices with a little of the juices.<br>Reheat: gently in gravy or stock so it does not dry out.<br>Keeps: 2/3 months frozen, 3 days in the fridge.</span>
