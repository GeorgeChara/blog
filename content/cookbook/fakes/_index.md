<h1>Fakes</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Greek lentil soup, one pot, 45 min, serves 6</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">brown lentils    400g
onion            1, chopped
garlic           4 cloves, sliced
carrot           1, chopped
tinned tomatoes  1 tin
bay leaves       2
dried oregano    1 tsp
olive oil        100ml
water            1.5L
salt and pepper  to season
red wine vinegar to finish
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Brown or green lentils only. Red split lentils collapse into mush and taste wrong.<br>No soaking, no stock. Water is correct here.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Rinse the lentils in a sieve and pick out any stones.

**2.** Everything except the olive oil, salt and vinegar into the pot. Bring to the boil, then simmer with the lid on for 35 minutes:

{{< ingr "brown lentils=400g, onion=1 chopped, garlic=4 cloves sliced, carrot=1 chopped, tinned tomatoes=1 tin, bay leaves=2, dried oregano=1 tsp, water=1.5L" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">That is the whole prep. One pot, nothing fried off first.</span>

**3.** Pour in the olive oil, turn the heat up and cook 5 minutes more, stirring.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The stirring emulsifies the oil into the soup and makes it creamy. Don't skip it.</span>

**4.** Fish out the bay leaves. Salt and pepper generously.

**5.** Serve, then add a teaspoon of red wine vinegar to each bowl at the table.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Vinegar in the bowl, never the pot, so everyone sets their own.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Serve with something salty. Feta, olives, or cured fish is traditional.<br>Better the next day. Keeps 4 days.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, freeze in portions for up to 3 months.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a pan, loosen with water. Add the vinegar fresh at the table.</span>
