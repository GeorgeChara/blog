<h1>Pasta with Tiny Meatballs</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Pallottine, 30 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">sausagemeat      400g
parmesan         40g, grated
tinned tomatoes  2 tins
garlic           3 cloves, sliced
olive oil        2 tbsp
spaghetti        400g
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">No egg, no breadcrumbs, no salt in the meatballs. Sausagemeat already has the binder and the seasoning in it.<br>Squeezing good sausages out of their skins beats a supermarket tub, which is mostly trimmings and fat ground to a paste. Slit them lengthways, about 3 minutes for 8.<br>A pinch of fennel seeds and chilli flakes pushes it towards Italian salsiccia.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Mix in a bowl, then pinch off chickpea sized bits and roll. About 50:

{{< ingr "sausagemeat=400g, parmesan=40g grated" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Roll the mix into a rope first and cut it into lengths. Far quicker than pinching from the bowl.</span>

**2.** Fry them in the oil in the Dutch oven for 3 minutes, shaking the pan rather than turning them.

**3.** Garlic in for a minute, then tip both tins of tomatoes straight in on top. Salt:

{{< ingr "garlic=3 cloves sliced, tinned tomatoes=2 tins, salt=to season" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Meatballs stay in the pot. No lifting out, no second bowl.</span>

**4.** Simmer 15 minutes, lid off.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lid off so it thickens. Don't go longer, small meatballs tighten up and go tough.</span>

**5.** Spaghetti in the big pan meanwhile.

**6.** Drain and tip it into the Dutch oven. Toss and serve out of the pot.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Small on purpose. They spread through the pasta so every forkful has one and you never need a knife.<br>This is the original that Italian American spaghetti and meatballs came from, pallottine, from Teramo in Abruzzo.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: the meatballs raw, spaced on a tray, then bag once solid.<br>Use: into the sauce from frozen, add 5 minutes to the simmer. Never freeze the pasta.</span>
