<h1>Shakshuka</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">serves 2, 30 min, one pan</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">onion              1, sliced
red pepper         1, sliced
garlic             2 cloves, sliced
olive oil          2 tbsp
cumin              1 tsp
paprika            1 tsp
chilli flakes      pinch
chopped tomatoes   400g tin
eggs               4
feta               50g, optional
parsley            handful
salt and pepper    to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Breakfast, lunch or dinner. Good with warm pita or crusty bread to mop the sauce.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oil in the pan on medium. Soften the onion and pepper, 8 to 10 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Take your time here, soft sweet peppers are the base of the whole dish.</span>

**2.** Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli. Cook 1 minute until fragrant.

**3.** Pour in the tomatoes, season, and simmer until thick, 10 to 15 minutes.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Reduce it well, or the eggs sit in a watery sauce.</span>

**4.** Make wells in the sauce and crack in the eggs. Lid on, low heat, 5 to 8 minutes until the whites set and the yolks are still runny.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Pull it when the whites are just set, they keep cooking in the hot sauce.</span>

**5.** Crumble over the feta and scatter with parsley. Serve from the pan.
