<h1>Apple Sauce</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">for roast pork, 15 min, makes a bowl</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">bramley apples   500g
water            2 tbsp
sugar            1 tbsp
butter           knob
lemon            1/2
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Bramleys are the proper apple, they collapse to a fluffy purée. Eating apples work but stay firmer and sweeter, so use less sugar.<br>Made for <a href="/cookbook/roast-pork-loin/">roast pork</a>.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Peel, core and chop the apples.

**2.** Into a pan, lid on, low heat about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until collapsed:

{{< ingr "water=2 tbsp, sugar=1 tbsp, lemon=1/2 squeezed" >}}

**3.** Beat smooth, or leave a few chunks. Stir in the butter and sweeten to taste.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Spice it: a pinch of ground cloves or cinnamon while it cooks.<br>The butter at the end makes it glossy, the lemon keeps the colour bright.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: in portions once cool.<br>Reheat: from frozen, gentle heat or a quick blast in the microwave.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen, 5 days in the fridge.</span>
