<h1>Cosmic Souvla Sauce</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">5 min, blend</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">parsley         large bunch
green chilli    1 to 2
garlic          1 clove
lemon           1
sunflower oil   4 tbsp
salt            to taste
msg (optional)  1/4 tsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">A green sauce for souvla, a parsley and lemon cousin of <a href="/cookbook/aji-verde/">aji verde</a>. Use the thinner parsley stalks too, they carry flavour, just trim the woody ends.<br>No fresh chilli? Green jalapeño hot sauce works, like green Tabasco, add it to taste. It is tangy, so go easy on the lemon.<br>Any neutral oil, sunflower or rapeseed, not olive which turns bitter blended.<br>A pinch of MSG with the salt rounds it out.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Blend until broken down:

{{< ingr "parsley=large bunch, green chilli=1 to 2, garlic=1 clove, lemon juice=1, salt=to taste" >}}

**2.** Stream in the oil with the blender running until it emulsifies into a smooth green sauce. Loosen with a splash of water if too thick.

**3.** Taste and adjust with more salt and lemon.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Best spooned over <a href="/cookbook/souvla/">souvla</a> or any grilled meat. For a squeezy bottle, blend it really smooth so it does not clog the nozzle, and shake before use.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Keeps 4 to 5 days in a sealed jar in the fridge. The top darkens as it oxidises, just stir it back in. It firms up cold, so let it sit a few minutes before squeezing. Don't leave it out at room temperature.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: in an ice cube tray, then bag the cubes.<br>Thaw: a cube or two as needed, or stir frozen into something hot.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen, colour dulls a little but the flavour holds.</span>
