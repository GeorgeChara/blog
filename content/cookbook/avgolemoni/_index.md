<h1>Avgolemoni</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">40 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">olive oil        15g
onion            1
carrot           1
celery           2 sticks
garlic           2 cloves
bay leaves       2
chicken breasts  2
chicken stock    1.4L
orzo             150g
lemon            1 to 2
egg yolks        2
dill             handful
salt and pepper  to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Orzo or short grain rice.<br>Off the boil before the eggs go in, or it curdles.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Dice and soften in the oil, 8 to 10 minutes:

{{< ingr "onion=1, carrot=1, celery=2 sticks" >}}

**2.** Garlic in for a minute, then simmer covered for 15 minutes:

{{< ingr "bay leaves=2, chicken breasts=2, chicken stock=1.4L" >}}

**3.** Lift out the chicken and bay leaves. Add the orzo and cook 10 minutes.

**4.** Shred the chicken and return it to the pot.

**5.** Whisk the egg yolks with the lemon juice. Whisk in a ladle of hot broth to warm them, then another.

**6.** Off the heat, pour the egg lemon back into the pot. Warm through gently, don't boil.

**7.** Stir in the dill, season, and adjust the lemon to taste. Serve.
