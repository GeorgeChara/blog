<h1>Thai Green Curry</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">One pot, 20 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">green curry paste  3 tbsp
coconut milk       2 tins, full fat
chicken thighs     600g, sliced
green beans        200g
fish sauce         2 tbsp
brown sugar        1 tbsp
makrut lime leaves 4
thai basil         handful
lime               1, juice
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Mae Ploy paste, from an Asian supermarket or online. The supermarket brands are weak and slightly bitter, and you end up using half a jar.<br>Lime leaves keep for months in the freezer and go in from frozen. Without them and the basil it is about 80% of the way there.<br>Lighter version: swap one tin for light coconut milk, or use two thirds of a tin topped up with water.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Spoon the thick cream off the top of one tin into the pot. Heat until it splits and the oil comes out.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Don't shake the tin. You want the solid cream, not the watery bit underneath.</span>

**2.** Fry the paste in that oil for 90 seconds, until the colour deepens.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">This is the step everyone skips. Paste straight into liquid tastes raw.</span>

**3.** Chicken in, stir to coat, 3 minutes.

**4.** Simmer 10 minutes:

{{< ingr "coconut milk=the rest, fish sauce=2 tbsp, brown sugar=1 tbsp, makrut lime leaves=4" >}}

**5.** Beans in for 4 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the lime juice and basil:

{{< ingr "green beans=200g, lime=1 juiced, thai basil=handful" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lime and basil go in off the heat or you lose them.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Go easy on the fish sauce until you have tasted it. The paste is already salty.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: cool fully, 3 months. The beans soften, so undercook them if you know it is going in the freezer.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a pan on low. Don't boil, the coconut milk splits.</span>
