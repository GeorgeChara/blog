<h1>Lime Cabbage Slaw</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">15 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Serves 4</span>

white cabbage      200g
red cabbage        100g
carrot             1
cucumber pickles   handful
fresh parsley      handful

<span style="color: #888;">Dressing</span>
lime               1
olive oil          2 tbsp
salt               to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Pairs with <a href="/cookbook/enchiladas/">enchiladas</a>.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Finely shred both cabbages and grate the carrot. Put in a large bowl.

**2.** Whisk together:

{{< ingr "lime juice=1, olive oil=2 tbsp, salt=to taste" >}}

**3.** Thinly slice the <a href="/cookbook/pickles/">cucumber pickles</a> and add to the bowl with the torn parsley. Pour over the dressing and toss well.

**4.** Rest 10 minutes before serving to let the cabbage soften slightly.
