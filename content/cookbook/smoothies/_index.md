<h1>Smoothies</h1>

<p class="smoothie-name">Green Machine</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">spinach              handful
banana               1
frozen blueberries   handful
ground flaxseed      1 tbsp
greek yoghurt        2 tbsp
water                to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make any day. Antioxidants from the blueberries, protein and good bacteria from the yoghurt.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Immune Hit</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">kale                 handful
ginger               thumb
turmeric             1 tsp
frozen mango         handful
chia seeds           1 tbsp
coconut water        to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make when feeling a bit rough. Ginger and turmeric fight inflammation, mango loads it with vitamin C.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Protein Recovery</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">spinach              handful
avocado              1/2
protein powder       1 scoop
nut butter           1 tbsp
banana               1
oat milk             to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make after a workout. Protein and nut butter rebuild muscle, banana refuels you.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Anti-Inflammatory Beet</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">cooked beetroot      1
turmeric             1 tsp
ginger               thumb
frozen strawberries  handful
chia seeds           1 tbsp
coconut water        to blend
black pepper         pinch
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">The pepper is not optional, the piperine drives turmeric absorption. Drink 2/3 hours before a ride for the nitrates.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Tropical Energy</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">spinach              handful
frozen mango         handful
frozen pineapple     handful
banana               1/2
coconut oil          1 tsp
coconut water        to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make when you need quick energy. Tropical fruit for fast fuel, coconut water to top up electrolytes.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Gut Health</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">kale                 handful
banana               1
almond butter        1 tbsp
ground flaxseed      1 tbsp
frozen cherries      handful
kefir                100ml
water                to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make when your gut needs a reset. Kefir brings live probiotics, flaxseed and banana feed the good bacteria.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Cold Brew Breakfast</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">cold brew            100ml
banana               1
protein powder       1 scoop
oats                 2 tbsp
peanut butter        1 tbsp
oat milk             to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make instead of breakfast. Caffeine to wake up, oats for slow energy. Soak the oats in the cold brew for 10 minutes first for smoothness.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Brain Smoothie</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">frozen blueberries   handful
walnuts              small handful
spinach              handful
ground flaxseed      1 tbsp
banana               1/2
green tea            to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make before deep work. Walnuts and flax for omega 3, blueberries and green tea for focus. Soak the walnuts in the cooled green tea for 10 minutes first.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Morning Refresher</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">frozen berries   handful
banana           1/2
lemon            squeeze
ginger           thumb
honey            1 tsp
water or juice   250ml
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make the night before. Light and hydrating, the lemon keeps it bright overnight. Fill the jar to the top and shake in the morning.</span>

<p class="smoothie-name">Ruby Booster</p>
<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">cooked beetroot      1
frozen berries       handful
frozen mango         handful
banana               1
ginger               thumb
lemon                squeeze
greek yoghurt        2 tbsp
coconut water        to blend
</pre>
<span class="smoothie-note">Make any day. The mango and yoghurt round off the earthy beetroot and add protein, the beetroot brings nitrates for circulation. Seeds out of the lemon, they go bitter.</span>
