<h1>Pastitsio</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">Cypriot baked pasta, makaronia tou fournou, 40 min bake at 180°C (160°C fan), serves 8</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Meat sauce</span>
pork mince       300g
beef mince       300g
onion            1, finely diced
garlic           2 cloves, crushed
ground cinnamon  1 tsp
ground cloves    pinch
ground nutmeg    pinch
dried mint       1 tsp
passata          185g
red wine         100ml
olive oil        30g
salt and pepper  to season

<span style="color: #888;">Pasta</span>
ziti lunghi      500g

<span style="color: #888;">Halloumi bechamel</span>
full fat milk    1 litre
butter           150g
plain flour      150g
halloumi         110g, grated
cheddar          60g, grated
parmesan         25g, grated
ground nutmeg    pinch
salt and pepper  to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Cypriot pastitsio uses pork, or a pork and beef mix, and halloumi in the bechamel where the Greek version uses kefalograviera.<br>Ziti lunghi are the long tubes this is made for. Penne rigate works at a push.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Soften the onion for 8 minutes, then the garlic for 1 minute:

{{< ingr "olive oil=30g, onion=1 finely diced, garlic=2 cloves crushed" >}}

**2.** Add both minces, break up the clumps, and brown all over, about 8 to 10 minutes. Pour in the wine and let it reduce for 3 to 4 minutes.

**3.** Add and simmer on low for 20 to 25 minutes, until thick and most of the liquid is gone. Season and set aside:

{{< ingr "ground cinnamon=1 tsp, ground cloves=pinch, ground nutmeg=pinch, dried mint=1 tsp, passata=185g" >}}

**4.** Cook the pasta in well salted water 1 to 2 minutes under the packet time. It finishes in the oven. Drain and set aside.

**5.** Make the bechamel. Melt the butter in a heavy pan over medium heat. Stir in the flour and cook 3 to 4 minutes, until it smells biscuity.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stand mixer: make it in the bowl set over a pan of simmering water, whisk attachment, milk drizzled in.</span>

**6.** Add the milk 200ml at a time, whisking between each. Keep over medium heat until it coats the back of a spoon. Off the heat.

**7.** Whisk in the cheeses and nutmeg:

{{< ingr "halloumi=110g grated, cheddar=60g grated, parmesan=25g grated, ground nutmeg=pinch, salt and pepper=to season" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The halloumi is salty, so taste before adding more salt.</span>

**8.** Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease a deep baking dish, about 32x22cm.

**9.** Lay the pasta in the dish, all the tubes facing the same way so it slices cleanly. Meat sauce over and level it, then the bechamel over that, levelled into a thick even layer.

**10.** Scatter a little grated halloumi on top. Bake 40 minutes until bubbling and deep golden.

**11.** Rest at least 15 minutes before cutting, it sets as it cools.
