<h1>Parsley Yoghurt</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">5 min, blend or stir</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">greek yoghurt   200g
parsley         large handful
garlic          1 clove
lemon           1/2
olive oil       1 tbsp
salt            to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Blend it for a smooth lime green sauce, or finely chop the parsley and stir through for a white sauce with green flecks.<br>Loosen with a splash of water if you want it drizzly.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Blend until smooth and green:

{{< ingr "yoghurt=200g, parsley=large handful, garlic=1 clove, lemon juice=1/2, olive oil=1 tbsp, salt=to taste" >}}

Or chop the parsley fine and stir everything together by hand.

**2.** Taste and adjust with more salt and lemon.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Top with <a href="/cookbook/pickled-red-onions/">pickled red onions</a> for a sharp, pink finish against the green.</span>

## Extras

Classic ways to take it somewhere:

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">mint              cooling, great with lamb
cumin + chilli    warm and smoky
tahini            richer, nuttier
sumac             tangy, lemony
grated cucumber   makes it tzatziki
dill / coriander  in place of the parsley
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Lovely with <a href="/cookbook/souvla/">souvla</a> wrapped in a warm <a href="/cookbook/flour-tortillas/">flour tortilla</a> with <a href="/cookbook/pickled-red-onions/">pickled onions</a>, a souvlaki style wrap.</span>
