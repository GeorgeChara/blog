<h1>Sausage Ragu</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">40 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">sausagemeat      500g, or 8 sausages
onion            1, chopped
garlic           3 cloves, sliced
fennel seeds     1 tsp
chilli flakes    pinch
tinned tomatoes  2 tins
whole milk       100ml
olive oil        2 tbsp
rigatoni         400g
parmesan         to serve
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The tiny meatballs with the effort taken out. Same ingredients, no rolling.<br>Rigatoni or another tube. The chunks lodge inside them.<br>Throw in a parmesan rind while it simmers if you have one.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Sausagemeat into the hot Dutch oven with the oil. Leave it completely alone for 5 minutes, then break it into big chunks and brown for another 10.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Don't stir it into mince. Left alone it browns properly and holds together in chunks, and the crust stuck to the bottom of the pot is most of the flavour.</span>

**2.** In for 5 minutes:

{{< ingr "onion=1 chopped, garlic=3 cloves sliced, fennel seeds=1 tsp, chilli flakes=pinch" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Aromatics go in after the browning. Any earlier and they burn while the meat is still catching.</span>

**3.** Milk in, simmer until it is almost gone, scraping the bottom of the pot as you go.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Milk before the tomatoes. It takes the acid edge off, and the scraping lifts all that browned crust back into the sauce.</span>

**4.** Tomatoes in. Simmer 25 minutes, lid off. Salt.

**5.** Rigatoni in the big pan, 2 minutes under the packet time. Save a mugful of the water before draining.

**6.** Pasta into the Dutch oven with a splash of the water and a handful of parmesan. Toss until it turns glossy.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The starch in that water is what makes the sauce cling instead of sliding off into the bottom of the bowl.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong><br>Freeze: the sauce only, 3 months.<br>Reheat: from frozen in a pan, loosen with a splash of water. Never freeze the pasta.</span>
