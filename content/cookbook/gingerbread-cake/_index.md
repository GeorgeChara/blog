<h1>Gingerbread Cake</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">35 min bake, 180°C (160°C fan), 23cm square tin, serves 9</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Dry</span>
plain flour          250g
bicarbonate of soda  5g
ground ginger        3g
ground cinnamon      3g
ground cloves        0.5g
salt                 1.5g

<span style="color: #888;">Wet</span>
black treacle        225g
hot water            180ml
unsalted butter      113g
brown sugar          67g
egg                  1
vanilla extract      5ml
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Black treacle is the British name for molasses, use either.<br>A 23cm square tin, a 9-inch springform, or an 11x7 all work. Not a round cake tin, there is too little batter for one.<br>Lovely with spiced cream cheese frosting: use the method on the <a href="/cookbook/carrot-cake/">carrot cake</a>, with a pinch of cinnamon, ginger and cloves stirred in.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 160°C fan. Grease a 23cm square tin.

**2.** Whisk the flour, bicarbonate of soda, ginger, cinnamon, cloves and salt.

**3.** In a jug, whisk the black treacle into the hot water until smooth. This loosens the treacle so it blends evenly and keeps the cake moist.

**4.** Beat the butter smooth, 1 minute, then beat in the brown sugar for 2 minutes. Beat in the egg and vanilla.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:-0.5em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Stand mixer: paddle, medium.</span>

**5.** Add the dry mix and the treacle water alternately in three goes, starting and ending with dry. Mix just until combined.

**6.** Pour into the tin and bake 35 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.

**7.** Cool completely before frosting.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: wrap the unfrosted cake well, or freeze slices.<br>Reheat: thaw at room temperature.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen, 5 days in a tin. It tastes even better the day after baking.</span>
