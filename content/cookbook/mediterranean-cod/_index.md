<h1>Mediterranean Cod</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">serves 4, 25 min, cast iron skillet</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Fish</span>
cod fillets        4 (about 150g each)
plain flour        3 tbsp
ground coriander   1 tsp
paprika            3/4 tsp
ground cumin       3/4 tsp
salt               3/4 tsp
black pepper       1/2 tsp

<span style="color: #888;">Sauce</span>
lemon juice        5 tbsp (about 2 lemons)
olive oil          5 tbsp, plus more for searing
butter             2 tbsp, melted
garlic             5 cloves, minced
parsley            handful, chopped
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The spiced flour is what lifts this above a plain bake, warm not hot, and it crisps in the sear.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Heat the oven to 200°C. Pat the cod dry.

**2.** Mix the lemon juice, olive oil and melted butter in a small bowl. Stir the garlic through and set aside.

**3.** Combine the flour, coriander, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper on a plate. Dip each fillet in the lemon mix, then dredge all over in the spiced flour.

**4.** Heat a good glug of olive oil in the cast iron skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Sear the cod 2 minutes a side until golden.

**5.** Pour the lemon garlic sauce over the fish and slide the whole skillet into the oven. Bake 8 to 10 minutes, until it flakes at the thickest part.

**6.** Scatter with parsley and serve with the pan sauce spooned over.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The cast iron goes hob to oven in one move, no second dish to wash. The handle comes out screaming hot, so leave a cloth over it as a reminder.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.5em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Serve over <a href="/cookbook/pourgouri-pilafi/">pourgouri pilafi</a> or couscous to catch the sauce.</span>
