<h1>Leftover Chicken Curry</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">20 min, serves 4</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">cooked chicken   300g, or whatever's left
onion            1, chopped
garlic           3 cloves, crushed
ginger           thumb, grated
curry powder     2 tsp
ground cumin     1 tsp
tinned tomatoes  1 tin
yoghurt          100g
spinach          200g
oil              2 tbsp
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Day two of the roast chicken. Uses about half the meat off the bird.<br>If you have browned onions and garlic ginger paste in the freezer, this is a ten minute job.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Soften the onion in the oil for 5 minutes, then the garlic and ginger for 1 minute:

{{< ingr "onion=1 chopped, garlic=3 cloves crushed, ginger=thumb grated, oil=2 tbsp" >}}

**2.** Spices in, stir for 30 seconds:

{{< ingr "curry powder=2 tsp, ground cumin=1 tsp" >}}

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Half a minute in hot oil is the difference between curry powder tasting like curry and tasting like dust.</span>

**3.** Tomatoes in, simmer 10 minutes.

**4.** Off the heat, stir in the yoghurt.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Yoghurt goes in off the heat or it splits.</span>

**5.** Back on low. Spinach until it wilts, then the chicken for 2 minutes, just to warm through.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin:0.2em 0 0.9em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The chicken is already cooked. Simmer it properly and it goes stringy.</span>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Eat within two days of roasting the bird, and only reheat it once.</span>
