<h1>BLT Baguettes</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">20 min, make ahead, serves 8</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;">baguettes        4
streaky bacon    12 rashers
tomatoes         3
little gem       1
butter           softened, to spread
mayonnaise       4 tbsp
salt and pepper  to taste
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Bake the <a href="/cookbook/baguette/">baguettes</a> ahead and cool completely before filling.<br>Soft bread is fine, the crust won't stay crisp wrapped.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Cook the bacon until crisp. Drain on kitchen roll and leave to cool completely.

**2.** Deseed and slice the tomatoes. Salt them lightly in a separate bowl, leave a few minutes, then blot dry on kitchen roll. Spin or pat the lettuce bone dry.

**3.** Split the cooled baguettes lengthways.

**4.** Build each one from the bottom up, amounts per baguette:

{{< ingr "butter=both cut faces, mayonnaise=1 tbsp, little gem=2–3 leaves, streaky bacon=3 rashers, tomato=3–4 slices, little gem=2–3 leaves on top" >}}

**5.** Wrap each tightly in greaseproof paper, then foil. Keep in the fridge or a cool bag. Cut each baguette in half to serve.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Keeps 3–4 hours in a cool bag.<br>Half a baguette per person.</span>
