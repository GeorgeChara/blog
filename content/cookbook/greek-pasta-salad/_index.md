<h1>Greek Pasta Salad</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">high protein, make ahead, serves 4, 20 min</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Salad</span>
pasta              300g
cooked chicken     300g
cherry tomatoes    250g
feta               200g
black olives       100g
cucumber           1/2
red onion          1/2

<span style="color: #888;">Dressing</span>
olive oil          5 tbsp
lemon              1
Greek yogurt       1 tbsp
dried oregano      1 tsp
garlic             1 clove
salt and pepper    to season
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">Chickpeas or fried halloumi swap in for the chicken. Use wholewheat or chickpea pasta to push the protein and fibre higher.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Cook the pasta a touch firmer than al dente, drain, rinse under cold water and toss with a little oil so it doesn't clump.

**2.** Shake the dressing together in a jar. The yogurt keeps it creamy and pourable straight from the fridge.

**3.** Combine, then toss through the dressing:

Hold a little dressing back, pasta drinks it up as it sits. Refresh with a squeeze of lemon at lunch.<br>Keeps 5 days in the fridge, airtight. Don't freeze, it thaws mushy. Out on the side: 1 hour max in the heat.</span>
