<h1>Brioche Buns</h1>
<p style="color: #888; margin-top: -0.5em;">16 min bake, 180°C (160°C fan), makes 8</p>

## Ingredients

<pre style="padding: 1em; border-radius: 4px; display: inline-block; margin: 0; color: #000;"><span style="color: #888;">Tangzhong</span>
strong white flour  20g
milk                100g

<span style="color: #888;">Dough</span>
strong white flour  400g
caster sugar        40g
fast action yeast   7g
salt                8g
milk                140g
egg                 1
unsalted butter     60g

<span style="color: #888;">To finish</span>
egg                 1
sesame seeds        to top
</pre>

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 2px solid #E5DECF; padding-left: 0.6em;">The tangzhong, a cooked flour paste, is what keeps them soft for days.<br>Warm the milk to lukewarm, not hot.</span>

## Recipe

**1.** Whisk the tangzhong flour and milk in a small pan. Heat, stirring, until it thickens to a paste. Cool.

**2.** In a stand mixer with the dough hook, mix the dough flour, sugar, yeast and salt. Add the milk, egg and tangzhong. Knead on low to bring together, then medium 5 minutes.

**3.** Add the butter a bit at a time, then knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and shiny.

**4.** Cover and prove until doubled, 1 to 1.5 hours.

**5.** Divide into 8, shape into tight balls, space on a lined tray. Prove 1 hour until puffy.

**6.** Egg wash and scatter sesame. Bake at 180°C (160°C fan) for 16 to 18 minutes until deep golden.

**7.** Brush with melted butter while warm.

<span style="display:block; color:#888; font-size:0.8em; margin-top:0.8em; border-left: 3px solid #8fcfe8; padding-left: 0.6em;"><span style="color:#3f9fd4; font-size:1.1em;">❄ <strong>Freezer</strong></span><br>Freeze: bake the full batch, cool fully, wrap each bun. Warm from frozen in the oven.<br>Bake once and freeze, dough balls prove unevenly from frozen.<br>Keeps: 3 months frozen.</span>
